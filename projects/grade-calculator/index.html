Weighted average

How it works

Each score is multiplied by its weight, then the contributions are added together. For example, a score of 90 at 40% and a score of 80 at 60% produce an average of 84%.

This practice calculator uses percentage scores only, without letter-grade conversions or extra credit. Your instructor’s grading rules determine your actual course grade. Entries are not saved.
