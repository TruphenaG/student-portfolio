Understanding the forecast

The completed-work average uses only finished assessments. The projection also includes your expected scores. The target calculation finds the weighted average needed across all remaining work; individual scores can differ.

This model assumes fixed weights totaling 100%, scores from 0–100%, and no dropped scores, penalties, or extra credit. It estimates outcomes; your instructor’s rules determine your actual grade.
